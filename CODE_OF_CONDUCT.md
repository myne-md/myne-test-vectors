# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Myne to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone who uses
it, reads the specification, or contributes to it, regardless of experience,
identity or background. This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages
and every other project space.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and specific in review. "This breaks the ratchet because X" is
  kind; "this is wrong" is not.
- Assuming the other person read the code and reached a different conclusion,
  and asking which part they read.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including on a design
  that took a long time to argue for.
- Crediting other people's work and respecting licences.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information, including their email address or
  the contents of a vault. A recovery phrase or vault content in an issue is
  the same class of harm as doxxing, however anonymised it looks.
- Misrepresenting a security property. Telling a user that data is safe on the
  basis of a guarantee the code does not make is a serious conduct problem
  here, not a technical disagreement.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when an individual is representing
the project in public. It covers this repository, its issue tracker, and the
companion repositories under the `myne-md` organisation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They will remove, edit or
reject comments, commits and issues that violate them, and explain the reason.
Responses escalate with the behaviour: a private word, then a warning, then a
temporary block, then a permanent one.

Report a concern privately to a maintainer or through security@myne.md. Reports
are handled privately, and a maintainer who is the subject of a report will not
participate in handling it.
